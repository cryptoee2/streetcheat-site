  • Your actual route. Closures on the roads you drive, not every closure in the city.

Shift work

Built for rotating shifts

Pick your pattern, tap your first day on, and StreetCheat knows every drive after that. Made for nurses, first responders and anyone on a rotation.

  • Ready-made patterns. From 1 on / 2 off to 7 on / 7 off, plus 2-2-3.
  • Or build your own. Any run of days on and days off.
  • Regular hours too. Or pick the same days every week, like Monday to Friday.

Alerts

A heads-up, not a flood

You hear about closures on the drives you make, and nothing about the rest of the city.

  • Quiet hours. Alerts wait until your quiet hours end. A leaving-soon reminder still gets through.
  • The evening before. An email for each route whose next drive has closures, at the time you choose.
  • Every Sunday. A week-ahead preview of every drive, even when they're all clear.

Questions

Good to know

Where does the closure information come from?

From the public data of the agencies that manage the roads: state transportation departments, toll roads, and city and county governments. See the full list, with a link to each one's official data.

Is StreetCheat a government app?

No. StreetCheat is an independent app made by Hantis USA, LLC. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any government agency. Closures can change without notice, so always follow posted signs and the agencies' own announcements.

Does StreetCheat track my location?

No. StreetCheat never uses your phone's location, only the addresses you type. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.

What does it cost?

It's free: up to 3 routes, each with email alerts to one address. When we add paid plans, our first 500 members keep that free plan, free for good.

When can I get it?

StreetCheat is coming soon to Android and iPhone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. Without the app, email support@streetcheat.com from the address on your account. More about deleting your account.
