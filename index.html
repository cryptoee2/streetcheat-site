StreetCheat

Know about closures before they happen.

StreetCheat watches the drives you make most (home to work, school drop-off, the weekly run to the job site) and tells you when a road closure is in your way, before you leave.

Coming soon to Android and iPhone

How it works

  1. Add a route once. Where from, where to, which days, and what time you leave.
  2. We check it every hour. Against official closure information from state transportation departments and city governments.
  3. You hear about it first. A notification when something is on your way, and if you like, an email summary the evening before.

What you get

  • Closures on your actual route, in your direction of travel, not every closure in the city.
  • Only the ones that matter for when you drive. A daytime lane closure won't bother you on a 5 AM commute.
  • A week-ahead look every Sunday, morning-of reminders, and quiet hours.
  • Rotating shifts: 1 on / 2 off, 4 on / 4 off and more.
  • Each route's alerts by email too, to you or someone who makes the same drive.

Free for our first 500 members

StreetCheat is free: up to 3 routes, each with email alerts to one address. When we add paid plans, the first 500 members keep that free plan, free for good.

Where it works

State highways in 47 states, plus city and county streets in Los Angeles, New York, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Philadelphia, San Antonio, San Diego, Dallas, Austin, Washington DC, Boston, Miami, Seattle, Denver, Detroit and more than 300 other cities and counties. Each route shows which sources it's checked against, and tells you plainly when an area isn't covered yet.

Private by design

StreetCheat never uses your phone's location, only the addresses you type. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.
