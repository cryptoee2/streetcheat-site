2. What StreetCheat does

StreetCheat checks routes you save against road-closure information that government agencies publish, about once an hour. When a closure looks like it affects a route at the time you drive it, we send a notification, and an email summary if you turn that on.

StreetCheat is a heads-up service. It does not give turn-by-turn directions, plan detours or tell you that a road is open.

3. Closure information: please read this

StreetCheat is not a government app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any federal, state, county or city agency. We pass on what agencies publish, and the data sources page lists them.

That information can be wrong, incomplete, late or missing:

  • Agencies can change, cancel or add closures without notice, or stop publishing data.
  • We check about once an hour, so a new closure may reach you late or not at all.
  • Matching a closure to your route and your driving time can miss a closure or flag one that doesn't affect you.
  • Confidence levels built from other users' reports and votes are opinions, not confirmations.

So please:

  • Treat an alert, or the lack of one, as a hint, not a promise. Road signs, traffic officers and the agency's own announcements always come first.
  • Never read or set up StreetCheat while driving. Pull over first, and follow the law where you are on phone use.
  • Plan extra time for important trips, and don't rely on StreetCheat for emergencies or anything where a delay could cause harm.
