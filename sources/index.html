Data sources

Updated October 7, 2026

StreetCheat reads public road-closure information published by the government agencies listed below, and links to each one's official data or closure page.

Not a government app

StreetCheat is an independent app made by Hantis USA, LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any federal, state, county or city government or transportation department. Closure information comes from these agencies' public data; it can change without notice, and the agency's own announcements and the signs on the road always come first.
